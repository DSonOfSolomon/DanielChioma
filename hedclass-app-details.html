HEdClass App

A web-based system that automates undergraduate degree classification, replacing manual spreadsheet-driven workflows used by academic administrators. The system applies complex academic rules to compute final award outcomes, including weighted multi-year performance, credit validation, overrides, and institutional constraints such as resit capping and progression requirements.
